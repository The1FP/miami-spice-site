Something wrong with a restaurant listing? Listings are compiled from the public Miami Spice pages on miamiandbeaches.com. Restaurants change their prices and serving days during the season, and the official pages are not always updated immediately. Always confirm with the restaurant before you go.

Common questions

A restaurant is missing. The app lists what the official pages list. If a restaurant joined recently, it may appear after the next data refresh.

The price or day shown is wrong. Please report it and call the restaurant to confirm in the meantime.

A restaurant has no map pin. A few listings have bad coordinates in the source data. They still appear in the list and in search.
