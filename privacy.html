Privacy Policy

Unofficial Miami Spice · last updated 14 August 2026

This app collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted to us, because there is no “us” to transmit it to — we operate no server that receives your data.

Location

If you grant location access, your coordinates are used entirely on your device, to centre the map and to sort restaurants by distance. Your location is never transmitted, never stored, and never associated with any identifier. You can decline, and every feature except distance sorting still works.

Data stored on your device

Your saved and “been there” lists, and your appearance preference, are stored locally using iOS’s standard preference storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

The app makes two kinds of outbound request:

  • Restaurant photographs are loaded directly from the image host used by miamiandbeaches.com. That host necessarily sees the request, as it would if you visited their website in a browser. Images are held in memory only and are not written to disk.
  • Dataset refresh — the app may fetch an updated restaurant list from a static file. This request contains no personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
