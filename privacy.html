Privacy Policy

Unofficial Miami Spice · last updated 15 August 2026

The app shows advertising, and advertising collects data. We operate no server and no account system, and we never receive your personal information ourselves. But the app includes Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising identifier and information about the ads you see. That is described in full below.

Advertising

The app displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To do this, the AdMob software in the app collects:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and other device-level identifiers.
  • Advertising data — which ads were shown to you, and whether you interacted with them.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and may be used for third-party advertising, including personalised advertising, and for measuring advertising performance. It may be used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. It is not linked by us to your name or to any account, because the app has no accounts.

Google's handling of this data is governed by their own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choice about tracking

Shortly after you first open the app, iOS will ask whether you allow the app to track you. You can say no. If you decline, advertising becomes non-personalised and the advertising identifier is not made available. Nothing else about the app changes, and no feature is withheld. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Location

If you grant location access, your coordinates are used entirely on your device, to centre the map and to sort restaurants by distance. Your location is never transmitted to us, never stored on a server, and is not used for advertising. You can decline, and every feature except distance sorting still works.

Data stored on your device

Your saved restaurants, your "been there" list, your dining companions, and your appearance preference are stored on your device using iOS's standard preference storage. Deleting the app deletes them.

iCloud sync

So that your saved lists follow you between your own devices, they are also written to Apple's iCloud key-value storage using your own iCloud account. This data goes to Apple, under your Apple ID, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see it. If you sign out of iCloud or disable iCloud for this app, the lists remain on the device and simply stop syncing.

Dining companions and your contacts

You can label a saved restaurant with the person you went with. When you pick someone, iOS shows the contact picker outside of the app — the app has no access to your address book and never asks for contacts permission. Only the name you explicitly choose is handed back to the app, and it is stored on your device with the rest of your saved data.

Network requests

Beyond advertising, the app makes these outbound requests:

  • Restaurant photographs are loaded directly from the image host used by miamiandbeaches.com. That host necessarily sees the request, as it would if you visited their website in a browser. Images are held in memory only and are not written to disk.
  • Dataset refresh — the app may fetch an updated restaurant list from a static file. This request contains no personal information.

Children

The app is rated 13+ and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, there is nothing for us to export or delete on request. To limit advertising data, decline the tracking prompt described above, or reset your advertising identifier in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. To remove locally stored data, delete the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
