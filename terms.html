Terms of Use

Menu Bite · last updated 7 September 2026

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive behaviour towards other people. Post either and your content is removed and your account can be ended without warning.

Most of this app needs no account and these terms barely touch you. They matter from the moment you write something other people can see.

Using the app

Menu Bite is free, and lists restaurants taking part in public restaurant-week and restaurant-month programmes. It is unofficial and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any of those programmes or their organisers. Menus and prices are collected from each programme's own published material and can be wrong or out of date — confirm with the restaurant before you go.

You may use the app for your own personal, non-commercial use. Do not scrape it, resell its data, or try to break, overload or work around its limits.

Your account

An account is optional and exists so you can rate a restaurant, write a review, follow people and invite somebody to join you. You are responsible for what happens under your account. Choose a username that is not offensive, does not impersonate somebody else, and is not a brand you do not own.

You must be old enough to agree to these terms where you live.

What you post

You keep ownership of what you write. By posting it you give us permission to show it inside the app and on pages the app links to, for as long as it is there.

You must not post anything that is:

  • abusive, harassing, threatening, or aimed at bullying a person
  • hateful towards anybody because of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, disability, sex, gender, age or sexual orientation
  • sexual, obscene, or violent
  • false about a real person or business, or written to damage them
  • somebody else's private information, including their name, address, phone number or photograph
  • spam, advertising, or a review of a place you have not been
  • somebody else's writing passed off as yours
  • illegal, or encouraging something illegal

A review is for the meal. It is not the place to describe a member of staff, another customer, or anybody's appearance.

How this is enforced

Filtering. Everything written in the app is checked before it is posted, and text that breaks the rules above is refused at the point of writing.

Reporting. Every review and every profile carries a way to report it. Reported writing is hidden immediately, before anybody looks at it, and is then re-checked automatically. It stays hidden if it broke the rules or if several separate people reported it; otherwise it is restored and put in a queue for a person to read.

Blocking. You can block anybody from their profile or from anything they wrote. Blocking hides them from you and you from them, in both directions, everywhere in the app. They are never told.

Ending an account. We may remove content and end an account that breaks these terms, without notice and at our discretion. Serious cases — anything targeting a person, or anything illegal — end an account on the first offence.

Getting in touch. Report anything through the app, or write to us at the address on the support page. We aim to answer reports promptly, and the automatic hiding described above means reported writing comes down straight away rather than waiting for a reply.

Your own data

What the app collects, and the much longer list of what it does not, is in the Privacy Policy. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.
